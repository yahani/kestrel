The problem

Checking competitor prices by hand doesn't last

Most small store owners know they should watch their competitors. In practice it means opening a dozen tabs every few days, and after a busy week it stops happening. Then a competitor drops a price or runs out of stock, and you find out from your sales numbers a week later.

Self-serve tools exist, but they start around $99 a month and expect you to set up and maintain the tracking yourself. When a competitor redesigns their site, the tracking quietly breaks.

Kestrel is the done-for-you version. I set it up, I fix it when a competitor's site changes, and the results come to your inbox.

How it works

Watch, spot, tell

  1. Every morning

    Watch

    Kestrel visits each competitor product page you chose, slowly and politely, and records the price and stock.

  2. Compared with yesterday

    Spot

    It flags price moves over 1%, items going out of or back in stock, and any competitor now cheaper than you.

  3. Only when something changed

    Tell

    You get one email listing every change. Quiet days send nothing. A Google Sheet with full history is optional.

Each client gets their own private setup. Your competitor list, price history and email settings are never shared with anyone else or stored in public.

Privacy and fair use

Polite by design

  • Public product pages only. No logins, no CAPTCHA workarounds, no personal data.
  • Kestrel follows each site's robots.txt and waits at least 5 seconds between requests to the same site.
  • You confirm in writing that the sites you want tracked allow automated monitoring. If you're not sure, I'll check.
  • Amazon and other large marketplaces are not supported.
  • Your setup runs in its own private space. Passwords and email settings are stored as encrypted secrets, never in files.
